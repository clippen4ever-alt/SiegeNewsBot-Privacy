# Privacy Policy – SiegeNewsBot

**Last updated:** November 18, 2026

## 1. About SiegeNewsBot

SiegeNewsBot is a bot for Root that automatically retrieves publicly available Rainbow Six Siege news from the Steam News API and posts announcements in a designated Root community channel.

## 2. Data Collection

SiegeNewsBot does not intentionally collect or store personal information about Root community members.

The bot retrieves publicly available news from Steam and processes announcement identifiers to prevent duplicate posts.

## 3. Data Storage

SiegeNewsBot stores identifiers of previously processed Steam announcements to avoid posting the same news multiple times.

The bot does not intentionally store personal information about Root community members or maintain a database of their messages.

The bot's hosting infrastructure may vary depending on deployment. This privacy policy will be updated to identify the hosting provider when the production deployment is finalized.

## 4. Third-Party Services

SiegeNewsBot uses the following services:

- **Root:** To publish announcements in community channels.
- **Steam:** To retrieve publicly available Rainbow Six Siege news.

The bot may use an external hosting provider depending on its deployment configuration.

These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

## 5. Data Sharing

SiegeNewsBot does not intentionally sell or share personal information about community members.

## 6. Data Retention

Previously processed Steam announcement identifiers are retained to prevent duplicate announcements.

No separate database of Root community members is intentionally maintained.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the bot's functionality, hosting infrastructure, or data processing practices change.

## 8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or SiegeNewsBot, please contact the bot developer through the designated Root support community.
